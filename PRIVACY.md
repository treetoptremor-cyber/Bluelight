# Bluelight privacy policy

Bluelight controls Philips Hue Bluetooth lights from your phone.

- **No account, no server.** The app has no sign-in and sends nothing over the internet.
- **Bluetooth.** It uses Bluetooth only to find and control your Hue lights. iOS asks for permission the first time.
- **Data on your phone.** Your lights' names, groups, presets and routines are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **No tracking.** There are no analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.
- **Diagnostics log.** A text log of Bluetooth activity is kept on your phone to help fix problems. It is never sent anywhere unless you share it yourself.

Bluelight is independent and not affiliated with Signify or Philips Hue.
